Privacy policy

What Savepoint Atlas collects, why, who else handles it, and how to get it back or delete it.

Who we are

Savepoint Atlas is made by Sogilex, run by [OPERATOR NAME], based in [COUNTRY]. For anything in this policy, contact [CONTACT EMAIL]. We are the controller of the personal data described here.

The short version

  • You can use the checklist without an account. It is then saved only in your browser.
  • With an account we store your email, your handle, your checklist and your settings, so they sync across devices.
  • We don't sell data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking cookies.
  • Friends see your completion percentages, never which items you ticked or what you wrote.
  • You can export everything or delete your account from the Account page at any time.

We don't ask for your name, address, date of birth or payment details. Card payments, when available, are handled entirely by Stripe on its own pages. We never see or store card numbers.

Stored in your browser

Signed out, your checklist and settings are kept in your browser's local storage. Signed in, your browser also keeps a sign-in session so you stay signed in. This is needed for the site to work, not for tracking. Clearing your browser data removes it.

What other people can see

  • Friends (only after both of you accept) see your handle and your completion percentages for verified pack entries. They don't see your ticks, your own items or your notes.
  • Everyone can see approved pack entries. We don't show who submitted an entry.
  • Reviewers on our team see submitted entries, including the source you gave, to decide whether to publish them.

Who else handles your data

We use these providers to run the site. Each processes data only to provide its service to us.

  • Supabase stores accounts and data. Data is hosted in [SUPABASE REGION].
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts the website and logs visitors' IP addresses for security.
  • Resend (Resend, Inc.) delivers sign-in emails. It receives your email address and the content of the sign-in email.
  • Stripe will process payments when paid features launch. Stripe acts under its own privacy policy for payment data.

Some of these providers are based in, or store data in, the United States. Where the law requires it, transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Legal basis (EU and UK users)

  • Contract: running your account, syncing your checklist and showing friends' progress.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the site secure, preventing abuse, and reviewing submissions so pack entries are accurate.
  • Consent: product emails, only if you switch them on. You can switch them off at any time.

How long we keep it

  • Account data stays until you delete your account. Deleting removes your profile, checklist, settings and friendships straight away.
  • Entries you submitted stay in the pack after you delete your account, but are no longer linked to you.
  • Copies may remain in our providers' backups for a limited period before they are overwritten.
  • Data kept only in your browser stays until you clear it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to or restrict how we use it. Most of this you can do yourself:

  • Export: Account page → Export my data.
  • Correct: change your handle and settings on the Account page.
  • Delete: Account page → Delete account.

For anything else, email [CONTACT EMAIL]. If you're in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority. California residents have rights under the CCPA. We don't sell or share personal information as those terms are defined there.

Children

Savepoint Atlas is not intended for children under 13, or under 16 in the EU and UK. Many of the games it covers are rated for adults. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll tell signed-in users before they take effect.
