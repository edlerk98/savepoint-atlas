Plate 01 · House rules

  1. Only submit what you've seen in the game yourself, or can point to a clip or screenshot of.
  2. A source is required. For example: "Played it, chapter 2, after the bank job", or a video link with a timestamp.
  3. Write it in your own words. Don't paste text from other guides or wikis.
  4. Mark spoilers honestly. Story twists are major spoilers.
  5. No guesses or leaks. If it isn't confirmed in the released game, don't submit it.
